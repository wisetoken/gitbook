# ✍️&nbsp;Signature Stakes

## What is a Signature Stake?

A **Signature Stake** is a WISE stake created with the **"I prefer this stake to be transferable"** option enabled when [staking WISE](Staking_WISE.md). Unlike a standard stake, a Signature Stake:

* Can be **transferred** to another wallet or used by third-party DeFi protocols that explicitly support the stake NFT
* Remains **scrapable** — interest can be collected at any time without affecting the principal
* Keeps earning its full staking APY and bonuses for the length of the stake, for durations up to **42 years**

## Signature Stakes as Collateral

Signature Stakes are transferable, verifiable on-chain positions. A native Wise collateral product is in development, with the aim of allowing holders to borrow against a stake without closing it.

**Borrowing against Signature Stakes is not yet available through WiseOne, and there is no live Signature Stake integration with Anvil.** Transferability alone does not make a stake accepted collateral. Supported integrations and borrowing terms will be documented when the product is released.

## The Largest WISE Staking Position

The Signature Stakes contract (`0x48791DbDFB38bAA58e48329148C321bF33B3c1e1`) is the single largest WISE staking position in the ecosystem — holding roughly a quarter of all staked WISE. See the live breakdown of top WISE holders on [DeBank](https://debank.com/token/eth/0x66a0f676479cee1d7373f3dc2e2952778bff5bd6/group).

## How to Create a Signature Stake

1. Go to [https://stake.wise.one](https://stake.wise.one) and click **Create WISE stake**.
2. Set your staking amount and duration as usual.
3. Turn on **I prefer this stake to be transferable**.
4. Confirm the transaction — your stake is now transferable. Borrowing against it is a separate, upcoming feature.

See [Staking WISE](Staking_WISE.md) for the full walkthrough of creating, scraping, and terminating stakes.
